Loop je ’s avonds alleen naar huis, of reis je in je eentje? Met SafeWalk deel je je reis tijdelijk met mensen die je vertrouwt.

## English
**Name:** SafeWalk – Home safe together
**Subtitle:** Share your trip with people you trust
**Short description:** Temporarily share your trip with trusted contacts. Check-in timer and SOS.
**Description:**
Walking home alone at night or travelling solo? SafeWalk lets you temporarily share your trip with people you trust.

• Start a trip: destination, expected arrival and who may follow along
• Live map for your contacts – only during that trip
• Check-in timer: confirm you're safe; if you don't in time, the server alerts your contacts
• Big SOS button with countdown and cancel; contacts receive your last known location
• See per contact whether an alert was sent, failed or is unknown
• Privacy first: sharing ends automatically, locations are deleted within 24 hours after your trip, admins never see your location
• Block and report abuse

Premium (€2.99/month): more contacts, 90-day history, custom check-ins, motion detection and an AI safety assistant. SOS, 2 contacts, temporary sharing and basic check-ins stay free.

Important: SafeWalk is not an emergency service and never calls 112/911 automatically. Notifications and location updates may be delayed or not delivered. In immediate danger, call 112 yourself.
